Everything you need to trust a supplier and pay an invoice

Attavo extends Workday from the inside. Turn it on, and the supplier records and invoices flowing through Financials and HCM are validated automatically as they arrive.

Verify

Validate and continuously monitor supplier data, and stop payment fraud before it starts.

  • Legal name, tax ID, and registered address verified against authoritative sources
  • Bank account and routing checks that catch redirected-payment fraud
  • Sanctions, denied-party, and watchlist screening for suppliers and their owners
  • Duplicate detection and continuous re-validation across the supplier lifecycle

Invoices

  • Reads PDF, email, scan, Excel, and CSV invoices in 100+ languages and every currency
  • Validates against master data, purchase orders, and government tax authorities

  • Clears the AP backlog without adding headcount

How Attavo works

From a single toggle to a validated payment

Attavo sits between Workday and the authoritative sources that prove a supplier and an invoice are real.

1

Connect with one toggle

Turn Attavo on inside Workday and flip a single secure trigger. It reads the supplier and worker events Financials and HCM already produce, with no schema changes and no data migration.

2

Validate in real time

When a supplier is created or an invoice arrives, Attavo checks the data in parallel against live government, tax, banking, and watchlist sources, returning results in minutes.

3

Route and resolve

Clean records flow straight through. Anything with a finding is sent back to the supplier or routed to the right reviewer, with the assistant ready to resolve it.

Built-in checks

The controls your auditors keep asking for

Every check runs automatically and repeats on a schedule and before every payment, so your data stays clean long after onboarding.

Sanctions screening

Suppliers and beneficial owners checked against OFAC, denied-party, and exclusion lists.

Bank verification

Routing and IBAN checks plus account-name matching to stop redirected payments.

Duplicate detection

Catch suppliers that share a tax ID or bank account before they reach the master file.

Supplier enrichment

Firmographics, ownership structure, and ESG scores added from trusted data providers.

Continuous monitoring

Records are re-validated on a schedule and re-checked automatically before each payment run.

Any-format capture

PDF, email, scan, Excel, and CSV invoices read with no templates to build or maintain.

PO & tax matching

Line-level matching against purchase orders and validation with government tax authorities.

Exception routing

Findings are routed to the right owner with the context needed to clear them fast.

Global coverage

Validation in 110+ countries, 100+ invoice languages, and every ISO currency.
